# Simulated Financial Impact

The synthetic pilot reduced modeled COPQ/application from **R18.66 to R8.96**. At the pilot sample volume, this is an illustrative unit-cost comparison only; it is not extrapolated to real-bank savings.

This is **not a real savings claim**. It is arithmetic applied to synthetic unit costs and synthetic process volumes. Required-control appraisal cost is excluded from the saving calculation.
