# Design of Experiments

A separate randomized, replicated **2³ full factorial** experiment tests staffing capacity, document pre-validation, and routing strategy. Baseline transactions are observational and are not labelled as DOE.

Model R²: **0.712**. Best observed factor combination: staffing=+1, pre-validation=+1, routing=+1, mean cycle time=3.70 h.

See `results/doe_results.json` for fitted coefficients and ANOVA p-values. Results are synthetic experimental evidence, not workplace outcomes.
