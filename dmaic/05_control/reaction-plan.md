# Reaction Plan

1. Confirm the signal is not a data-quality or measurement issue.
2. Review special-cause evidence: outage, staffing, reviewer availability, demand surge.
3. Contain backlog using approved capacity-routing rules without bypassing KYC controls.
4. Record cause, action, owner and closure date.
5. Re-establish limits only after a verified stable process shift; never move limits merely to hide poor performance.
