# Interview Guide

**Why this process?** Retail onboarding exposes time, quality, queueing, control and customer CTQs, making it suitable for demonstrating DMAIC without using confidential data.

**Primary CTQ?** Time to activation is the customer-facing time CTQ, while FPY/rework is the stronger baseline improvement problem because aggregate SLA compliance mixes three specifications.

**How was MSA handled?** A separate categorical Attribute Agreement study uses 60 items, four reviewers and two repeated trials. Agreement to reference was 87.7% and Fleiss' κ 0.681.

**Why not ordinary Cp/Cpk?** Cycle time is strongly right-skewed and has one-sided tiered upper specifications. Stability is assessed first; empirical conformance and tail percentiles are primary capability evidence within each tier.

**How did you avoid calling correlation causation?** The data-generating-process map identifies upstream drivers and mediators. Regression omits queue-time mediators and reports conditional associations; experimental conclusions come from a separate randomized DOE.

**Why DOE?** It tests intervention factors and interactions under randomized replicated conditions rather than inferring treatment effects from the observational baseline.

**How is COPQ governed?** Required manual review is appraisal/control cost. Only internal and external failure costs are counted in COPQ.

**What would change in a real bank?** Operational definitions, cost assumptions, risk-control rules, measurement-system thresholds and capability specifications would need governance approval and calibration against representative production data.
