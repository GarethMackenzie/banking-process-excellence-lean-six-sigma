# Limitations

- The process is synthetic and is not calibrated against a real bank's operating model.
- KYC workflow, staffing and cost assumptions are deliberately simplified.
- MSA, DOE and pilot data are synthetic demonstrations of methodology, not measured workplace outcomes.
- Aggregate SLA compliance combines different governed specifications and must not be used as a standalone capability claim.
- Special-cause detection is imperfect by design; missed events and false alarms are reported rather than hidden.
- The regression is observational and cannot identify causal effects by itself.
- No legal or regulatory conclusion is implied by the fictional risk/control logic.
- The project does not include a production queueing engine, production identity verification, real PII, or a production Power BI deployment.
